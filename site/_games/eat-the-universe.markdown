Eat The Universe is one of two mobile games I shipped as Unity engineer at Singularity Forge, released on Android and iOS.

A cosmic arcade game where you play a ravenous galactic entity, devouring asteroids, planets, and stars to grow — exploring 8 unique galaxies across 64 levels while dodging rival devourers.
