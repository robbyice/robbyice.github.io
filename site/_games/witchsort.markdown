Witchsort is one of two mobile games I shipped as Unity engineer at Singularity Forge, released on Android and iOS.

A cozy, witch-themed color-sort puzzle game — pour and sort potions by color across relaxing, hand-painted levels with no timers or penalties, set to a soothing soundtrack.
